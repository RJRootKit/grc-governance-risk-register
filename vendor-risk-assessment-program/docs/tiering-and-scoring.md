# Tiering and Scoring

## Tiers
| Tier | Criteria | Assessment | Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Critical | Processes personal or financial data, or business-critical service | Full questionnaire, evidence, contract clauses | Annual |
| 2 Important | Limited data access | Short questionnaire | Every 2 years |
| 3 Low | No data access | Basic onboarding check | At renewal |

Tier by data sensitivity first. Over-questioning low-risk vendors burns goodwill.

## Scoring
Each answer scores 0 (no), 1 (partial), 2 (yes with evidence). Vendor score = total / maximum.
- 80%+ Acceptable
- 60-79% Acceptable with remediation plan
- Below 60% Escalate to risk owner

## Contract minimums (Tier 1)
Security requirements, breach notification timeline, audit rights, sub-processor controls, data return and deletion.
