# Governance Structure

## Policy hierarchy
1. **Information Security Policy** (board-approved, annual review)
2. **Topic policies**: access control, acceptable use, incident response, data protection, supplier security
3. **Standards**: password, encryption, logging, hardening baselines (CIS/STIG)
4. **Procedures and work instructions**

## Roles
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Board / Risk Committee | Sets risk appetite, approves policy, reviews top risks |
| CISO / vCISO | Owns the ISMS, reports risk posture |
| Risk owners | Accept, treat, or transfer assigned risks |
| Data Protection Officer | DPA 2019 obligations, DPIAs, breach notification |
| Internal audit | Independent assurance |

## RACI (extract)
| Activity | Board | CISO | Risk owner | DPO | Audit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Approve policy | A | R | C | C | I |
| Risk assessment | I | A | R | C | I |
| Treatment plans | I | C | A/R | C | I |
| Breach notification | I | A | C | R | I |

## Review cadence
Policies annually; risk register quarterly; management review at least twice a year (ISO 27001 clause 9.3).
