# Risk Methodology

## Scoring
Risk score = Likelihood (1-5) x Impact (1-5)

| Score | Rating | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1-4 | Low | Accept, monitor |
| 5-9 | Medium | Treat within 6 months |
| 10-16 | High | Treat within 90 days, owner reports monthly |
| 17-25 | Critical | Immediate treatment, board visibility |

## Risk appetite (example, set your own)
- No acceptance of Critical risks without board approval
- High risks accepted only with documented rationale and a review date

## Treatment options
Mitigate, Transfer (insurance, contract), Avoid, Accept.

## Residual risk
Re-score after controls are in place. A risk is never closed without evidence.
